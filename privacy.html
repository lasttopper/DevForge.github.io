Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains what information VYROX (“we”, “us”) collects when you use this website, and how we use it. The short version: we only use what you send us to reply to you.

Information we collect

  • Details you submit through our forms, such as your name, business name, website URL, and email or WhatsApp contact.
  • Any message or project details you choose to share with us.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry or deliver the service you requested (for example, a website audit or project quote).
  • To keep a record of our correspondence about your project.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We don’t send marketing emails unless you ask us to.
  • We don’t use invasive tracking or advertising technologies on this site.

Data retention

We keep inquiry details only as long as needed to handle your request and any follow-up work. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. Contact us at hello@vyrox.studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page. Material changes will be highlighted here.
